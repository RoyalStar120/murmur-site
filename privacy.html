Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Murmur gives each person on a team an AI agent that sits in on their meetings, keeps track of their tasks and does routine work for them. This policy explains what Murmur collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

What Murmur collects

  • Your account: your name, email address, role and a hashed password. Murmur never stores your password itself.
  • Meetings you choose to record: the transcript, the notes, tasks and decisions Murmur writes from it, and text read from screens shared in the meeting. Recording only happens after everyone is told. The agent announces itself in the call and anyone can ask it to leave.
  • What you tell your agent: your skills, availability and any notes you add. Notes you mark private stay with your agent and are never shared with anyone else.
  • Google data, only if you connect Google (see below).

How Murmur uses Google data

Connecting Google is optional, and you can disconnect at any time. If you connect, Murmur asks for these permissions and uses them only for the purposes listed:

  • Google Calendar, read only (calendar.readonly): Murmur reads your upcoming events (title, time, attendees, Google Meet link and out-of-office status). It uses them so your agent can join your meetings, prepare agendas, and know when you're away. Murmur never changes your calendar.
  • Gmail, send only (gmail.send): Murmur sends emails from your address only when you approve them, or for the kinds of email you've told your agent it may send on its own (for example, reminders to yourself). Murmur cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): Murmur creates your team's task tracker spreadsheet and adds rows to it after meetings.
  • Your Google email address (email, openid): Murmur uses it to show which account is connected.

Limited Use. Murmur's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Murmur uses Google data only to provide the features you can see in the app.
  • Murmur never sells Google data, never uses it for advertising, and never uses it to train AI models.
  • People can't read your Google data except with your permission, for security or legal reasons, or when you ask for help.

AI processing

To write meeting notes, draft messages and run the agent meeting, Murmur sends meeting text and task information to an AI service provider, which processes it only to return a result. Murmur does not use your Google data or your meetings to train AI models.

Sharing

  • Within your team: your agent shares meeting notes, tasks and availability with your teammates' agents. It never shares private notes.
  • Service providers: the AI provider above, the meeting-bot provider that joins calls, and email delivery. Each gets only what it needs to do its job.
  • Murmur never sells personal information.

Where data is kept and for how long

Murmur stores data on the server your team runs Murmur on. Google sign-in tokens are kept only on that server and are never sent to your browser. Data is kept while your account is active. When you disconnect Google, Murmur deletes its Google tokens. Ask us to delete your account and Murmur deletes your account, notes and agent memory.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google: do it in Murmur (Agent activity → Connected apps), or remove Murmur's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Set what your agent may do: for each kind of work, choose Auto, Ask or Never.
  • Ask us to see or delete your data at the contact below.

Children

Murmur is built for workplaces and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell users in the app.
