Murmur

Your company's AIs all work alone. Murmur makes them work as a team.

Every employee gets an AI agent. The agents sit in on meetings, talk to each other, split up the work and handle repeated tasks, while people approve anything important.

An agent for every person

Each agent knows its person's role, skills, meetings and tasks, and joins their calls as a visible note-taker.

The agents meet

Every morning they compare notes and catch work nobody owns, duplicate work and deadlines at risk.

They do the routine work

Follow-ups, agendas, reminders and tracker updates, all within the limits each person sets.

People stay in charge

Anything sent to a client, or involving money, waits for a person's approval. Every action is logged.

How Murmur uses Google

If you choose to connect your Google account, your agent can:

  • Google Calendar: see your upcoming meetings, so your agent can join them, prepare agendas and know when you're out of office. It can also put follow-up meetings on your calendar at a time everyone's free, with your OK.
  • Gmail (send only): send the follow-ups, agendas and reminders you allow, from your own address. Murmur cannot read your inbox.
  • Google Sheets: create and update your team's task tracker.
